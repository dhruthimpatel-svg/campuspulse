# CampusPulse ⚡

### Your digital companion for campus life.

**CampusPulse** is a centralized digital platform built for **BNMIT students** to discover and stay updated with what's happening around campus — from clubs and events to announcements and useful student resources.

Instead of searching through scattered notices, club pages, and different communication channels, CampusPulse brings important campus information together in one accessible place.

## ✨ What CampusPulse Offers

* 📅 **Upcoming Events** — Discover hackathons, workshops, competitions, cultural events and more.
* 🏛️ **Club Discovery** — Explore BNMIT clubs and learn what they offer.
* 📢 **Announcements** — Stay updated with important campus updates.
* 📚 **Student Resources** — Access useful academic and campus-related resources.
* 🔎 **Smart Filtering** — Find relevant events and information faster.
* 📍 **Campus Information** — Easily explore campus-related details and locations.
* 📊 **Campus Dashboard** — Get a quick overview of available events, clubs and resources.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed to work smoothly across devices.

## 🎯 The Problem

Campus information is often distributed across multiple platforms and communication channels. Students can easily miss events, announcements, club activities or useful resources.

**CampusPulse aims to solve this information-fragmentation problem by creating one centralized digital campus hub.**

## 💡 Why I Built It

CampusPulse was built around a simple question:

> **“What if students could find everything happening on campus in one place?”**

The project combines a clean student-focused interface with a structured data layer to make campus information easier to discover and navigate.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **Backend / Database:** Supabase
* **Authentication & Access Control:** Supabase Auth + Row Level Security
* **Deployment:** Manus
* **Data:** Campus events, clubs, announcements and resources

## 🚀 Future Scope

CampusPulse can evolve into a complete campus ecosystem with features such as:

* 🔔 Personalized event notifications
* ❤️ Event bookmarking
* 🗓️ Personal student calendars
* 🤝 Club recruitment and membership
* 📍 Interactive campus navigation
* 📊 Student engagement analytics
* 🤖 AI-powered campus assistant
